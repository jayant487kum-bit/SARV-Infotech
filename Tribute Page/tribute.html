A Mind
Beyond
Numbers.

Srinivasa Ramanujan was one of the most remarkable mathematicians in history. Born on 22 December 1887 in Erode, India, he developed a deep fascination with mathematics from an early age.

What makes his story extraordinary is that he learned much of advanced mathematics independently. Without the advantages of extensive formal training, he produced remarkable mathematical discoveries involving number theory, infinite series, continued fractions and mathematical analysis.

His mathematical talent eventually brought him to Cambridge, where he worked with the renowned mathematician G. H. Hardy. His ideas continue to influence mathematics more than a century later.

03 — TIMELINE

His Remarkable Journey

1887

Born in India

Srinivasa Ramanujan was born on 22 December in Erode, in the Madras Presidency of British India.

1903

Mathematical Passion

As a teenager, he became deeply absorbed in advanced mathematics and began exploring it independently.

1913

The Letter to Hardy

Ramanujan sent his mathematical work to G. H. Hardy at Cambridge, changing the course of his life.

1914

Cambridge

He travelled to England and began his famous collaboration with G. H. Hardy.

1918

Royal Society

Ramanujan was elected a Fellow of the Royal Society, a remarkable achievement for a mathematician so young.

1920

His Legacy Begins

Ramanujan died on 26 April at the age of 32, leaving behind mathematical ideas that continue to be studied.
